Research · Teaching · Responsible AI

Hi, I'm Tasfia Mashiat.

I am an Assistant Professor of Instruction in Computer Science at the University of Iowa. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from George Mason University, where I worked with Prof. Sanmay Das.

My research focuses on machine learning, causal inference, algorithmic fairness, and resource allocation, with applications to societal challenges such as eviction prevention and policing.

Research & Projects

Selected work

Equitable Resource Allocation in the Context of Homelessness

Research on statistically efficient approaches to equitable allocation of homelessness services across population subgroups.

Fair Assignment of Policing Resources

A causality-based approach for allocating limited policing resources fairly across neighborhoods, evaluated on synthetic and real-world data.

A Deep Neural Network Approach for Retrofitting Word Embeddings

Deep learning for identifying morphologically related word embeddings in languages with rich morphology.

Examining 4G/5G LTE Cellular Network Infrastructure

Research testbeds for examining communication among user equipment, base stations, and the core network.

Examining the Patterns of Taxi Rides in NYC

Exploratory data analysis and machine learning to investigate taxi demand patterns in New York City.
